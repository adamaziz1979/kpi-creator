Definitions 7 measures
Net revenue per lead
Net commission (gross minus clawbacks) ÷ leads created. The latest quarter is provisional until its clawbacks have had 90 days to land.
Lead-to-sale conversion
All sales in a product area ÷ leads created in that area, in the same month. Includes the 6.1% of sales with no lead attached.
Cost per acquisition
Marketing spend ÷ net sales (sales not clawed back), by channel. Counter-metric: net revenue per marketing dollar.
Speed to contact
Median minutes from a lead arriving to the first call.
90-day clawback rate
Health and life sales clawed back within 90 days ÷ all health and life sales, by quarter of sale. Final quarters only.
Lead mix
Leads in each product area ÷ all leads, by month.
Complaints per 1,000 sales
Complaints ÷ sales × 1,000 over the last 12 months.
