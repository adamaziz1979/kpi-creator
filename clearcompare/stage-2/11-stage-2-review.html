5. A fix made during this run

The first run showed paid search costing about $4,650 per sale. That was a bug in the sample data generator, not in Kewpie's calculation: marketing spend was scaled to full size while leads and sales were at 1-in-50 sample size. It's fixed, and the data was regenerated with the same seed. Every number on this page comes from the corrected data.
