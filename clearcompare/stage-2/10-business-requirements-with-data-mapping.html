Objective: Grow net commission revenue profitably

Net revenue per lead · Top 5 · Must Have · Lagging

Serves: Grow net commission revenue profitably; Explain performance changes within a day

FieldDetail
What do we want to know?How much net commission we earn from each lead, after clawbacks
Why?It's the headline the CCO is asked about ("Revenue per lead dropped 9%"). Net, not gross, shows whether growth is profitable
ActionsIf below threshold 2 weeks running, use the revenue bridge to find the driver. The CCO then assigns the fix: marketing for mix or volume, the vertical head for conversion or clawbacks. Owner: CCO
How is it measured?Net commission ÷ leads created, weekly and quarterly, by vertical
Data mapping and calculationssales.gross_commission, sales.clawback_amount, sales.sale_date, sales.vertical · leads.lead_id, leads.created_ts, leads.vertical
Calc: SUM(gross_commission − clawback_amount) / COUNT(lead_id)
Conditions and filtersRevenue by sale_date, leads by created_ts. The 6% of unmatched sales are included (they carry a vertical). Clawbacks land up to 90 days after a sale, so the latest quarter is shown as provisional, with a clawback-adjusted figure beside it (agreed at Stage 2)
Target (SMART)Back to the April to June 2026 level by 30-Jun-27
Timing to confirm with the CCO: within 3, 6 or 9 months, or 1 or 2 quarters
· 3 months: by 31-Jan-27
· 6 months: by 30-Apr-27
· 9 months: by 31-Jul-27
· 1 quarter: by 31-Mar-27 (end of the January to March quarter)
· 2 quarters: by 30-Jun-27 (end of the April to June quarter)
ThresholdsGreen: at or above target · Amber: up to 3% below · Red: more than 3% below

Lead mix by product area · Sub-KPI under Net revenue per lead · Must Have · Leading

Serves: Grow net commission revenue profitably; Explain performance changes within a day

FieldDetail
What do we want to know?What share of leads comes from each vertical
Why?Verticals pay very different commissions, so a shift in mix moves revenue per lead without anyone selling worse
ActionsIf a low-commission vertical's share rises more than 3 points in a month, marketing reviews campaign targeting. Owner: Head of Marketing
How is it measured?Leads by vertical ÷ all leads, monthly
Data mapping and calculationsleads.vertical, leads.created_ts
Calc: COUNT(lead_id WHERE vertical = v) / COUNT(lead_id)
Conditions and filtersCalendar month by created_ts. Test leads excluded (none in the sample data)
Target (SMART)Energy leads at or below 30% of all leads each month, by 31-Mar-27 (moved from 31-Dec-26 at Stage 2). Energy was about 26% of leads until June 2026, then about 40% in July to September 2026
ThresholdsGreen: 30% or less · Amber: over 30% up to 33% · Red: over 33%

Cost per acquisition, by channel · Top 5 · Must Have · Lagging

Serves: Grow net commission revenue profitably (cut the cost of winning a sale through paid search)

FieldDetail
What do we want to know?How much marketing spend it takes to win each net sale, by channel
Why?Growth only helps if it isn't bought with rising acquisition cost. The CCO named paid search
ActionsIf a channel is above threshold for 2 weeks, marketing moves budget to the cheaper channels. Owner: Head of Marketing
How is it measured?Marketing spend ÷ net sales, monthly, by channel
Data mapping and calculationsmarketing_spend.cost, marketing_spend.channel, marketing_spend.date · sales.lead_id → leads.channel, sales.clawback_amount
Calc: SUM(cost) / COUNT(sales WHERE no clawback) per channel
Conditions and filtersNet sales = sales not clawed back. Unmatched sales (6%) can't be tied to a channel, so they're excluded and counted. Spend and sales are matched by month
Target (SMART)Paid search 10% below its July 2025 to June 2026 average by 30-Jun-27; other channels hold. The size of the cut is Gary's proposal
Timing to confirm with the CCO: within 3, 6 or 9 months, or 1 or 2 quarters
· 3 months: by 31-Jan-27
· 6 months: by 30-Apr-27
· 9 months: by 31-Jul-27
· 1 quarter: by 31-Mar-27 (end of the January to March quarter)
· 2 quarters: by 30-Jun-27 (end of the April to June quarter)
ThresholdsGreen: at or below target · Amber: up to 10% above · Red: more than 10% above

90-day clawback rate · Top 5 · Must Have · Lagging

Serves: Grow net commission revenue profitably (reduce commission clawed back within 90 days)

FieldDetail
What do we want to know?What share of health and life sales are clawed back within 90 days
Why?Finance's explanation of the fall: "Partners claw back commission if a health or life policy is cancelled within 90 days." Clawbacks also point to poor-fit sales
ActionsIf a vertical is above threshold for a month, its vertical head and compliance review the sales mix and call quality. Owner: Vertical heads
How is it measured?Clawed-back sales ÷ all sales, health and life, by the month of sale
Data mapping and calculationssales.clawback_date, sales.sale_date, sales.vertical
Calc: COUNT(clawback_date − sale_date <= 90 days) / COUNT(sales)
Conditions and filtersHealth and life only (the other verticals have no clawbacks). Only months whose sales are at least 90 days old count as final; newer months are shown as provisional
Target (SMART)Back to the July 2025 to June 2026 average by 30-Jun-27
Timing to confirm with the CCO: within 3, 6 or 9 months, or 1 or 2 quarters
· 3 months: by 31-Jan-27
· 6 months: by 30-Apr-27
· 9 months: by 31-Jul-27
· 1 quarter: by 31-Mar-27 (end of the January to March quarter)
· 2 quarters: by 30-Jun-27 (end of the April to June quarter)
ThresholdsGreen: at or below target · Amber: up to 1 point above · Red: more than 1 point above

Objective: Convert leads more efficiently

Lead-to-sale conversion, by product area · Top 5 · Must Have · Leading

Serves: Convert leads more efficiently

FieldDetail
What do we want to know?What share of leads become a sale, in each vertical
Why?Conversion moves before revenue does, so it gives an early warning
ActionsIf a vertical is below threshold for a week, its vertical head reviews contact speed, scripts and team coaching. Owner: Vertical heads
How is it measured?All sales in a product area ÷ leads created in that area, in the same month
Corrected by Kewpie, 20-Oct-26. Gary's draft was: leads that became a matched sale ÷ leads created
Data mapping and calculationssales.vertical, sales.sale_date · leads.vertical, leads.created_ts
Calc: COUNT(sales WHERE vertical = v) / COUNT(leads WHERE vertical = v), by month
Conditions and filtersSales by sale_date month, leads by created_ts month. Unmatched sales (6.1%) are now included, because every sale carries its product area. Sales usually land 0 to 6 days after the lead, so the monthly match is close
Target (SMART)Health 11% (stated by the CCO). Other verticals hold their July 2025 to June 2026 average, by 30-Jun-27
Timing to confirm with the CCO: within 3, 6 or 9 months, or 1 or 2 quarters
· 3 months: by 31-Jan-27
· 6 months: by 30-Apr-27
· 9 months: by 31-Jul-27
· 1 quarter: by 31-Mar-27 (end of the January to March quarter)
· 2 quarters: by 30-Jun-27 (end of the April to June quarter)
ThresholdsGreen: at or above target · Amber: up to 1 point below · Red: more than 1 point below

Complaints per 1,000 sales · Sub-KPI under Lead-to-sale conversion · Must Have · Lagging

Serves: Convert leads more efficiently

FieldDetail
What do we want to know?How many complaints we get for every 1,000 sales, by vertical
Why?"We're financial services: complaints and compliance breaches must stay visible." Conversion must not be bought with mis-selling
ActionsAny month above threshold triggers a compliance review of that vertical's sales calls. Owner: Head of Compliance
How is it measured?Complaints ÷ sales × 1,000 over the last 12 months, by product area
Changed at Stage 2: complaint numbers are too small for a monthly rate

Calc: COUNT(complaints) / COUNT(sales) × 1000
Conditions and filtersComplaints by complaint date, sales by sale_date, same month. Judged on a rolling 12 months; the monthly count is shown for context
Target (SMART)At or below the July 2025 to June 2026 rate by 30-Jun-27
ThresholdsGreen: at or below the July 2025 to June 2026 rate · Amber: up to 25% above · Red: more than 25% above

Speed to contact · Top 5 · Must Have · Leading

Serves: Convert leads more efficiently

FieldDetail
What do we want to know?How many minutes it typically takes from a lead arriving to the first call
Why?Leads go cold fast. It's the quickest lever the sales team controls
ActionsIf the median goes over threshold on any day, sales operations adjusts rostering across Sydney, Melbourne and Manila. Owner: Head of Sales Operations
How is it measured?Median minutes from lead created to first contact, daily, by team
Data mapping and calculationsleads.created_ts · contacts.first_contact_ts, contacts.agent_team
Calc: MEDIAN(first_contact_ts − created_ts) in minutes
Conditions and filtersLeads never contacted are excluded from the median and counted separately. Manila outcomes are blank before Mar-25, but contact times are still usable
Target (SMART)Median of 10 minutes or less by 31-Mar-27
Timing to confirm with the CCO: within 3, 6 or 9 months, or 1 or 2 quarters

Objective: Explain performance changes within a day

This objective is met by net revenue per lead together with a revenue bridge on the dashboard. It isn't a separate KPI.

FieldDetail
What do we want to know?Why net revenue moved between last quarter and this quarter
Why?"When a number moves, the dashboard tells me why before anyone asks." "Show me a bridge from last quarter to this quarter."
ActionsThe CCO uses the bridge in the Monday exec meeting to assign the fix to the right owner
How is it measured?The change in net revenue split into 4 effects: lead volume, vertical mix, conversion and clawbacks. The 4 effects add up to the total change
Data mapping and calculationsThe same fields as net revenue per lead, lead mix, lead-to-sale conversion and the 90-day clawback rate. Each effect is calculated by changing one driver at a time, holding the others at last quarter's values
Conditions and filtersAs net revenue per lead. The latest quarter's clawback effect is provisional until clawbacks have had 90 days to land
