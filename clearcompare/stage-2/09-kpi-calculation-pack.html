What the numbers say:

  • Lead mix explains most of the fall. Net revenue fell $26,253 between last quarter and this quarter. The shift into energy leads cost $29,658. Lower conversion cost another $12,186. More leads added $12,172 (see the revenue bridge below).

2. The dataset

In short: 5 tables of seeded sample data covering 24 months, from 1-Oct-24 to 30-Sep-26, built at 1-in-50 scale of ClearCompare's real volumes.

How the tables link

  • leads: one row per enquiry (58,509), with its product area, marketing channel, campaign, state and time
  • contacts: the first call for each lead (one per lead, 58,509), with the team that called and the outcome
  • sales: one row per sale (7,647), with commission and any clawback. 6.1% have no lead attached
  • complaints: one row per complaint (32), linked to a lead
  • marketing_spend: daily spend by channel and product area (13,115 rows). It links to leads by channel and product area, not lead by lead

What stands out

ObservationWhy it matters
Health is the biggest source of leads (40.9%), but energy is the biggest source of sales (50.9%)Energy converts well (about 24%) but earns little per sale. This is the lead-mix effect behind the fall
Commission per sale: median $104, mean $459, maximum $2,200Very lopsided: many small energy sales and a few large home loans. Averages need splitting by product area
245 sales (3.2%) were clawed back, always for the full commissionClawbacks are rare overall but concentrated in health and life
8.0% of call outcomes are blank, all Manila before March 2025A known gap. Contact times are still complete
Only 32 complaints, 81% about energyToo few for monthly rates. Energy's share of complaints is worth watching as energy grows
Spend per day and channel: median $19.69, maximum $366.09Paid search and social are the expensive channels

Not in the data: customer identities, the reason a policy was cancelled, agent names, or staff costs. Cost per acquisition covers marketing spend only.

3. Revenue bridge: last quarter to this quarter

Net revenue moved from $429,119 (April to June 2026) to $402,866 (July to September 2026), a fall of $26,253. Each effect is worked out by changing one driver at a time, in this order: leads, mix, conversion, revenue per sale. The four effects add up to the total.

EffectChange in net revenueWhat it means
Lead volume+$12,172212 more leads (7,474 to 7,686)
Lead mix−$29,658Energy rose from 27.0% to 39.5% of leads; health fell from 41.4% to 33.9%, life from 18.6% to 13.6%
Conversion−$12,186Mostly health: 11.0% down to 9.5%
Revenue per sale, after clawbacks+$3,419Mostly timing: this quarter's clawbacks haven't all landed
Total−$26,253Net revenue per lead: $57.41 down to $52.42 (−8.7%)
