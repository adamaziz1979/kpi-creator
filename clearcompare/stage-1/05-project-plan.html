Test case. Linked to Business Requirements v1.0 (20-Oct-26). Dates assume a start on 21-Oct-26 with 2-week sprints.

1. Goal and scope

  • Goal: a trusted weekly view that explains why net revenue per lead moves, using the Top 5 KPIs and the revenue bridge
  • In scope: the KPIs and sub-KPIs in the Business Requirements, the revenue bridge, the exec view and the daily vertical view
  • Out of scope: agent-level views, partner reporting, and the production Tableau build (this is the design prototype)

  • Weekly: the CCO and exec team review the Top 5 and the bridge every Monday
  • Monthly: owners review the sub-KPIs and thresholds
  • Quarterly: targets are re-checked against pace needed. Any changes go into the Business Requirements change log
