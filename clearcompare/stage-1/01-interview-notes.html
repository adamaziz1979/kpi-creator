Date: 20-Oct-26 Interviewer: Gary Stakeholder: Priya, Chief Commercial Officer (played by Ovy from stakeholder-file.md) Questions used: 7 of 7

Q1 (Why / What). What's the business problem, and why does it matter now?

"Revenue per lead dropped 9% last quarter. Marketing blames sales, sales blames lead quality, and finance says it's clawbacks. I need to know which."

Probe: What happens if nothing changes in the next 6 months?

I don't know.

Q2 (What). What are the 2 or 3 outcomes the business is trying to achieve, and how would you know each one is happening?

- Grow net commission revenue profitably
- Convert leads more efficiently
- Explain performance changes within a day, not a week
"On numbers: cut cost per acquisition on paid search, lift health insurance conversion back to 11% (it's 9.4% now), and reduce 90-day clawbacks."

Q3 (Who). Who will use this, and what decisions will they make with it?

- "Me and the exec team, weekly, in the Monday exec meeting"
- "Vertical heads and marketing, daily"

Probe: What format: screen, phone or board pack?

I don't know.

Q4 (Why / Action). For each outcome, what do you want to know, and what would you do if it went off track?

- "When a number moves, the dashboard tells me why before anyone asks."
- On revenue: "Partners claw back commission if a health or life policy is cancelled within 90 days. Reporting today shows gross revenue."
- On specific actions and who would act: I don't know.

Q5 (When). What targets and dates are you working to?

- Health conversion back to 11%
- Other target values and dates: I don't know

Q6 (Where). What data exists today, and what do you trust or not trust about it?

- Leads, contacts, sales (with clawbacks), marketing spend by day and channel, and complaints. 24 months of history. Tableau and Snowflake
- "6% of sales can't be matched back to a lead (manual partner uploads). Manila call outcomes were in a separate system before Mar-25."

Q7 (Constraints). Anything I must or mustn't do: privacy, tools, chart likes and dislikes?

- "We're financial services: complaints and compliance breaches must stay visible."
- "Agent-level data is fine for team leaders but not on the exec view."
- "Show me a bridge from last quarter to this quarter. I hate funnels that don't add up."
