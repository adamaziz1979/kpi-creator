2. Problem Statement

Net revenue per lead fell about 9% last quarter, and nobody can say whether the mix of leads, sales conversion or cancelled policies caused it. Without one trusted answer, teams argue about the numbers instead of fixing the problem.

The business problem in detail

  • As the CCO put it: "Revenue per lead dropped 9% last quarter. Marketing blames sales, sales blames lead quality, and finance says it's clawbacks. I need to know which."
  • As Gary understands it:
    • Net revenue per lead fell about 9% in July to September 2026
    • Nobody can split the fall into its causes: the mix of leads, sales conversion, clawbacks (commission partners take back when a policy is cancelled) and lead volume
    • Each team argues from its own gross-revenue report, so the exec team debates the cause instead of acting on it
