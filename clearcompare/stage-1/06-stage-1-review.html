Change 2: Timing options added to the net revenue per lead target

Where: Business Requirements, Top 5 KPI "1. Net revenue per lead", Target row, and the same row in Business Requirements with Data Mapping

Net revenue per lead: target
Before"Back to the April to June 2026 level by 30-Jun-27"
After"Back to the April to June 2026 level by 30-Jun-27"
Timing to confirm with the CCO: within 3, 6 or 9 months, or 1 or 2 quarters
· 3 months: by 31-Jan-27
· 6 months: by 30-Apr-27
· 9 months: by 31-Jul-27
· 1 quarter: by 31-Mar-27 (end of the January to March quarter)
· 2 quarters: by 30-Jun-27 (end of the April to June quarter)

How the dates are worked out: months are counted from 1-Nov-26. Quarters are full calendar quarters after the current one (October to December 2026). The current proposal, 30-Jun-27, is the same as the 2-quarter option.
