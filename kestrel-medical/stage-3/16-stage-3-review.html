1. The story

Checked against the requirements. Gary recommended leading with Kewpie's note C10: the data doesn't support "lost on price". The Problem Statement says Kestrel "believes price is the reason but can't yet prove or disprove it" and needs evidence before asking global for more discount room. Why we lost, from the debriefs (a built sub-KPI) answers that directly, so it can lead. The evidence is Strong Inference (scores parsed from the debrief letters), and the page says so under the headline. It says "the debriefs point to", not "service caused the losses".

BeatSentence
HeadlineKestrel lost 3 of its last 5 big tenders, and the buyers' debriefs point to service and support, not price: it was the biggest scoring gap on all three.
Under itPrice was the biggest gap on only 3 of 20 scored losses. Discount on big deals has held at 17.03% since 2024; revenue is 6.8% down on last year. Strong Inference (debrief scores).
What changedDiscount on big deals rose from 14.05% to 17.03% in 2024, and Kestrel still lost 3 of its last 5 big tenders.
WhyService and support was the biggest gap on all 3 big losses; price was never the biggest. Across all 20 scored losses, price was the biggest gap on only 3, service and support on 7. The 3 big losses went to 3 different winners, one a distributor that bundles brands.
What to doThe 3 actions below.

Read aloud without the charts, the four beats make sense on their own: the losses, the discount that didn't buy wins, the debrief evidence, the actions.

Colour. Monochrome blue throughout. The scoring areas are 6 categories, so they use the Charcoal and teal category colours in fixed slots (service and support teal, price coral, then clinical fit, supply reliability, training, sustainability), and keep the same colour on both charts they appear in. Status colours appear only on the two rated tiles, always with an icon and a word.

No barbell. The only two-period comparison by category is the net price realisation stand-in (2026 against 2025, 10 product groups). Ten categories exceed the 6 category slots and the changes are under half a point, so it stays as bars with the change in the label and the "See data" table.

Every chart has hover detail, a "Why it matters" line on hover or focus (from the "Why?" column), a "See data" table, animation on first view (click to replay, off with reduced motion), and works in dark and light mode.

5. Checks run

  • First screen at 1280 × 800: header, headline, the 3 action cards and the Top 5 tiles (with both stand-ins) end at 793px. Measured in the Browser pane
  • No detail line wraps at 1280px. No sideways scroll at 375px (page width 375). Sticky header switches off on phones
  • No console errors (Standard and Experimental A)
  • Lost tender values recalculated with Kewpie's correction C7 match Who is winning the tenders we lose (K4.3) for every winner (asserted in the build script)
