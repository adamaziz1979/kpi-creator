Definitions 11 KPIs · 4 stand-ins
Gross margin per cent
(Net sales minus cost of sales) ÷ net sales, shipped lines, January to December. Phase 2: waiting for cost of sales from SAP product costing. Must reconcile to finance's 41% before use.
Average discount off list price on big deals
1 minus net value ÷ list value, value-weighted, tender contract orders, by order date. Counter-metric: tender contract net sales and the big tender win rate.
Revenue growth on the year
Net sales shipped year to date ÷ the same months last year, minus 1, ship date. The 4% for 2027 is measured on 2026 full-year actuals.
Big tender win rate
Tenders won out of the last 5 decided tenders with a contract value of $5m or more. Annual amounts are multiplied by the contract length. Counter-metric: number and value of big tenders bid.
Monthly reports that match the agreed set of numbers
Reports whose monthly net sales check against the governed sales table, ÷ reports that carry a sales number. Phase 2: waiting for finance's agreed definitions.
