Test case, Sample data. Proposals only, for Adam's yes. Nothing in the Business Requirements v1.0, the Project Plan or spec.json has been changed, and Sharon hasn't been contacted.

Adam asked for placeholders for the KPIs that can't be built in this first pass, marked for Phase 2, where Kewpie works with a data engineer to source the data. Four KPIs are affected: Gross margin per cent (K1), Gross margin by product group (K1.1), Monthly reports that match the agreed set of numbers (K5) and Our price against the winning price on lost tenders (K4.1). None is dropped or reprioritised: each keeps its name, target and owner, and gets a placeholder tile now and the real calculation in Phase 2. Each placeholder sits beside a small stand-in that Kewpie calculated. A stand-in is labelled "Stand-in, not the KPI" and never takes the KPI's name, target, status colour or finance's stated 41 per cent. This file merges four of my earlier 11 rows (rows 1, 4, 5 and 9 in "02 Gary Response to Challenge Notes"), so Adam reads one consistent set. My other seven earlier rows (2, 3, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11) stand as written.
