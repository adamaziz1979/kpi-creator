C10: the data doesn't support "lost on price"

  • What it shows (Strong Inference, Sample data, 3 tenders): service and support was the biggest scoring gap on all three big losses. On Highlands Ambulance we were 1.5 per cent cheaper than the winner. Across 20 scored losses, price was the biggest gap in only 3
  • Does it change the requirements? No. Sharon's confirmed words were that she wants "the evidence to prove or disprove it". The Problem Statement and the tender measures were built to answer exactly that, and they have. Changing the statement now would put words in her mouth
  • What it does change:
    • Viktor's story should lead with it, because it's the answer Sharon asked for, even though it isn't the answer she expected
    • It backs the tighter discount target proposed for C5. More discount room may cost margin and still not win the tender
    • The Project Plan risk "The evidence may show losses weren't mainly price" (R2) has now happened. Sharon needs to hear it from Adam at the walk-through, not find it on a tile
    • New open question for Sharon: "The debriefs point to service and support. Do you want a service measure under Big tender win rate (K4) in the next release?" I wouldn't add one without her. She raised no service objective, and that's her call to make
