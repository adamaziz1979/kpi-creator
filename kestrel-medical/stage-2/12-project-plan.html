Phase 2, Source missing data, is a separate, new project after Stage 5 (section 8), planned and approved on its own.

1. Goal and scope

  • Goal: a first trusted dashboard Sharon can take to global as evidence and use in rep meetings, covering gross margin, revenue growth, big tender wins and one agreed set of numbers
  • In scope: the Top 5 measures and their six sub-KPIs in Business Requirements v1.3. Built where the data supports it; placeholder tiles with labelled stand-ins for Gross margin per cent, Gross margin by product group, Monthly reports that match the agreed set of numbers, and Our price against the winning price on lost tenders. KPIs with an open target (Average discount off list price on big deals, Big tender win rate) are shown without a target or status colour
  • Out of scope this time: CRM win rate, reps' reported competitor price cuts, service and field measures. Sourcing the missing data, all open questions and the open targets go to the Phase 2 project (section 8)

7. Review rhythm after go-live

  • Monthly: sales leader reviews the Top 5, in line with today's monthly reports (Sharon's expectation, to confirm)
  • After each big tender decision: bid manager updates the outcome, winning price and debrief
  • Before each global review: pack produced from the dashboard (date and format open)
  • Quarterly: finance report owner checks the monthly reports against the agreed set of numbers, until the Phase 2 project's automated monthly check replaces it
