2. Problem Statement

Kestrel has lost three of its last five big tenders, each over five million dollars, and believes price is the reason but can't yet prove or disprove it. With gross margin at about 41 per cent against global's 43 per cent target for 2027, and thirty monthly reports that give different answers, the business needs one set of evidence before it asks global for more discount room.

The problem as stated

Sharon, in her words: lost three of the last five big tenders, each over five million dollars, "never happened to me in 22 years". Reps say Kestrel is being undercut 10 to 15 per cent. She wants a win-loss view and pricing against the competition, so she can take evidence to global and argue for more discount room. And she wants out of Excel: thirty monthly reports, and half of them say different things.

The problem as Gary understands it

  • The ask and the target pull against each other. The ask is evidence for more discount. Global's target is more margin (about 41 to 43 per cent in 2027) and 4 per cent revenue growth, after a flat year
  • The loss reasons are belief, not yet evidence. They come from reps and one procurement person. Sharon's own account of the three losses: one "was price" (Southern Metropolitan Health Network), one was "about the stretcher as much as anything" (Highlands Ambulance, a 15-year customer), and one went to a distributor that bundles several brands (Western Plains Health District, won by Allied Clinical Supply)
  • The evidence exists but hasn't been used. Deb Harlow, the bid manager, keeps the tender file and the debrief letters
  • Nobody agrees on the base numbers. Thirty monthly reports from ERP extracts disagree, and Sharon spends three days a month reconciling them

Gary's note: the question I'd put to Sharon is "What decision changes if this number moves?" Here, the answer is the discount case to global. If the debriefs say Kestrel lost on product fit or bundling, more discount won't win the next tender and will cost the margin target. That's my reading, not Sharon's words, and Sharon has not confirmed it.
