1. Goal and scope

  • Goal: a first trusted dashboard Sharon can take to global as evidence and use in rep meetings, covering gross margin, revenue growth, big tender wins and one agreed set of numbers
  • In scope: the Top 5 measures and their six sub-KPIs in Business Requirements v1.0
  • Out of scope this time: CRM win rate, reps' reported competitor price cuts, service and field measures

7. Review rhythm after go-live

  • Monthly: sales leader reviews the Top 5, in line with today's monthly reports (Sharon's expectation, to confirm)
  • After each big tender decision: bid manager updates the outcome, winning price and debrief
  • Before each global review: pack produced from the dashboard (date and format open)
  • Quarterly: finance report owner checks the monthly reports against the agreed set of numbers
