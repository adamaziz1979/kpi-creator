Main points and action items

Main points

  • The trigger: Kestrel lost three of its last five big tenders, each over five million dollars. A first in Sharon's 22 years
  • The ask: a win-loss view and pricing against the competition, as evidence to argue to global for more discount room. And out of Excel: thirty monthly reports, half say different things
  • The tension: global wants 43 per cent gross margin in 2027 (about 41 now) and 4 per cent revenue growth. More discount pulls against the margin target
  • Price is a belief, not yet evidence: Sharon's corrected words are that she believes it's price, and wants the evidence "to prove or disprove it". One loss was "about the stretcher as much as anything", and one went to a distributor that bundles brands
  • Evidence holders: Deb Harlow (bid manager) keeps the tender file and debrief letters; Tom Nguyen (finance) owns the Excel reports and the margin numbers
  • Audience: Sharon makes the decisions. Main use is a pack for global, plus screen in rep meetings, monthly. Not phone
  • Data: SAP ERP, a field service system, a CRM (not trusted for win-loss), Excel, and the bid manager's tender spreadsheet. Detail sits with Tom and Deb
  • Top 5 proposed: Gross margin per cent; Average discount off list price on big deals; Revenue growth on the year; Big tender win rate; Monthly reports that match the agreed set of numbers
  • Confirmed by Sharon: the margin, growth and tender goals, the decisions, monthly use and the pack-first format. Not confirmed: the win-rate target (left for Deb) and the monthly reports target (left open). Thresholds and owners are Gary's proposals
