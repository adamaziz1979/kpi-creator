Key facts (Sharon's words)

  • Lost three of the last five big tenders, each over five million dollars
  • Gross margin about 41 per cent; global wants 43 per cent in 2027, plus 4 per cent revenue growth. Revenue flat last year
  • Financial year January to December, set by global
  • Reps say Kestrel is undercut 10 to 15 per cent "from what they're seeing"
  • Losses: Southern Metropolitan Health Network, ED stretchers, 2024 ("I was told it was price"); Highlands Ambulance, powered stretcher renewal, 2025 ("about the stretcher as much as anything"); Western Plains Health District, acute care beds, 2025 (lost to Allied Clinical Supply, a distributor that bundles brands)
  • Deb Harlow, bid manager: tender file and debrief letters. Tom Nguyen, finance: owns the Excel reports and the margin numbers
  • Systems: SAP ERP, field service system, CRM (not trusted for win-loss), Excel, tender spreadsheet. Monthly reports from ERP extracts
  • Three days a month reconciling thirty reports
  • Pack for global first, screen in rep meetings second, not phone. Monthly
  • Must speak to how Kestrel's market works, not global's standard view; automated and consistent

Gary's reading

  • The real problem is a discount decision being made on belief. The debriefs may show product fit or bundling, not price. If so, more discount costs margin and still loses
  • The Top 5 balances the margin side (gross margin, big-deal discount) against the win side (tender win rate), so the dashboard shows the trade-off rather than one side of it
