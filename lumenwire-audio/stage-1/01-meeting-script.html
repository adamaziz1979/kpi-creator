Main points

G'day Gary. The problem is simple to state. Revenue went from A$118.4m in FY25 to A$80.6m in FY26, down about a third. The board says they were blindsided, and Ros wants to see the business properly, not just a quarterly P&L. If nothing changes for six months? I'd be guessing, and I don't want to guess in front of the board. You'd have to ask Priya Anand, our CFO, for the forecast view. I can tell you what I'd fear: that we keep being surprised by a number we should have seen coming.
