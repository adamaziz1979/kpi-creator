Revenue for the last 12 months is A$80.5m, A$4.36m below the path to A$115m.

It peaked at A$122.1m in Jun-24 and dropped through FY26. Since Jun-26 it has been flat, while the path rises A$1.43m a month. Even the FY27 board plan, A$92.7m, would sit 5.2% below the path at Jun-27. So what took a third off revenue?

OEMs buying fewer units than they installed took A$48.0m off revenue.

Revenue fell A$37.8m, from A$118.4m in FY25 to A$80.6m in FY26. End customers installing 3.3% fewer devices cost only A$1.8m. The one A$7.4m licence and A$4.5m less paid in rebates softened the fall. Nothing is left unexplained.

Why

Page 3 · Largest cause first

OEMs ordered about twice what end customers installed, then stopped.

From Jul-24 to Apr-25 OEMs ordered 412,052 units a month while end customers installed 210,839. Lead times had fallen from about 48 weeks to 8, so there was less reason to hold stock. Since May-25 orders match installs (1.03 times), and hardware has held near A$13.4m a quarter.

Software grew to A$25.1m only because of one A$7.4m licence.

Without it, software fell 15.6%, from A$21.0m to A$17.7m. Other perpetual licences took A$3.52m off, from 105 deals to 81. Subscriptions added only A$0.23m and A$0.05m. So the FY26 software growth rests on one contract, and the FY27 plan repeats it.

About a third of design wins ship, and the share stops rising after three years.

Of 430 design wins from Jan to Jun 2023, 21% had shipped at 18 months and 32% at 42. The 80% belief was never measured. Today 31.5% of wins old enough have shipped, against 35%. Since Jul-24 reps earn A$400 per win with no clawback: wins rose from 230 to 273 a quarter, but the shipping rate stayed flat.

    Page 5 · New devices activated and forecast accuracy

    End customers kept installing; it was OEM buying that fell, and neither forecast saw it.

    New devices activated is the closest view of end-customer demand. It barely moved while OEMs cut their buying by 47%. Stock at the OEMs that report is still high, so buying may stay low. Both forecasts missed that step, and the CFO is still to choose which one counts, so both are shown.

    Signed off 27-Oct-26: new devices activated by the Head of Platform Engineering, forecast accuracy by the CFO.

    Installs fell 1.4% over 12 months, but the latest quarter is up 4.9%.

    Every quarter of FY26 was down on the year before, by 2.7% to 4.1%. Jul to Sep 26 turned up again, in 19 of 21 countries. The dip began the month firmware 4.5.0 shipped, so Engineering needs to rule that out. The cause is not settled: Working Hypothesis.

    New devices activated, change on the same quarter a year before. 12 months: 2,492,460 against 2,528,350. Target +5% by 30-Jun-27. Sample is 1 in 10 devices.

    Reporting OEMs hold 10.9 months of stock, down from 15.4 at the peak.

    Cover jumped when OEMs stopped buying, peaking in 2025-Q3. Their stock has since fallen 45% like for like, but purchases stayed low, so cover is still above the 9-month red line. More destocking is possible before hardware buying recovers.

    Total revenue, last 12 months, against the path to A$115m
    Sum of ledger revenue over the last 12 months (hardware sales less rebates, software licences and subscriptions, developer kits and freight), each month, against a straight line from A$80.6m (Jun-26) to A$115m (Jun-28). Owner: CEO. Counter-metric: gross margin %.

    Devices joining a network for the first time in the last 12 months, against the 12 months before, earliest date per device. Sample is 1 in 10 devices, counts × 10. Owner: VP Sales. Signed off by the Head of Platform Engineering, 27-Oct-26.
    Software revenue growth without one-off large licences
    Software revenue over the last 12 months ÷ the 12 months before, minus 1, leaving out any single licence over A$1m (to agree with the CFO). Growth including them shown alongside. Owner: CEO.
    Design wins that ship
    Of design wins at least 18 months old, the share that have reached shipping. Lost after design win counts as not shipped. Records with no stage dates are left out and counted. Owner: VP Sales.
    Forecast accuracy
    Each quarter, (forecast − actual) ÷ actual, using the forecast made on or before the first day of the quarter, for Finance and Sales. Owner: CFO. Signed off by the CFO, 27-Oct-26; Finance and Sales both shown until the CFO chooses one.
    Months of stock cover at the OEMs that report
    Units on hand at the OEMs that reported in the quarter (reels × 500) ÷ their average monthly units invoiced in the same quarter. Owner: VP Sales.
    Share of software revenue from the largest licences
    Largest licence (and largest five) as a share of all software contract value started in the last 12 months. Owner: VP Sales.
    Gross margin %, last 12 months
    (Revenue − cost of sales) ÷ revenue over the last 12 months. Target 76% (the board's FY27 figure). Owner: CFO.
    Months from design win to first shipment
    Median months between design win and shipping, for design wins at least 18 months old. Target set after the baseline. Owner: Head of Platform Engineering.

    Count of opportunities reaching design win in the financial quarter. Read with design wins that ship. Owner: VP Sales.
