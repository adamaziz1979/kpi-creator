The page answers Marcus's main question. The headline says "The business isn't yet growing underneath the fall", and it tests his story rather than telling it: the hardware fall is OEMs buying ahead (A$48.0m), software growth is one A$7.4m licence, and 31.5% of design wins ship against his unmeasured 80%.

The most important gaps:

  1. One number for Finance and Sales, so the board isn't surprised again (items 21, 35, 36). Both forecasts are shown and both miss badly (Finance 23.2%, Sales 34.3% over four quarters). The CFO hasn't chosen which counts, and there is no forward view. Owner: CFO, then Kewpie and Viktor.
  2. Targets other than A$115m read as firm on the page (items 8, 26). Marcus said "Don't take my word for those numbers". The 0%, 35%, +5% and 10% targets are signed off by the acting SME, not by the CFO or VP Sales, and the tiles don't mark them as proposals. Owner: Gary, with SME sign-off and the CFO.
  3. Where to put sales and engineering effort (items 12, 16, 22). One of Marcus's three decisions. The page has no split of installs or design wins by region, OEM or rep, and nothing separates "the product or the sales effort" behind software. Owner: Kewpie for the splits, Viktor for the view; OEM names wait on the CFO and legal.
  4. The board told which actions are being taken each month (item 24). The Top 3 actions have owners and targets, but no monthly status. Owner: Viktor, with the CEO supplying the status.
  5. One page in the board pack, and phone use (items 19, 20). The draft is a 6-page edition; page 1 carries all three outcomes, but whether page 1 stands alone as the board-pack page isn't confirmed, and nobody has asked the chair about phones. Owner: Jennifer, after Marcus confirms at Stage 5; the chair via the CEO for phones.
