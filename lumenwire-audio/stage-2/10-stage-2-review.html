2. Problem Statement

Revenue fell by about a third in a year, from A$118.4m to A$80.6m, and the board was caught by surprise because nobody puts the Finance, Sales and Engineering numbers together. Nobody can yet show, from one set of numbers everyone trusts, whether the business is still growing underneath the fall.

As stated by Marcus: Revenue went from A$118.4m in FY25 to A$80.6m in FY26, down about a third. The board says they were blindsided, and Ros (chair) wants to see the business properly, not just a quarterly P&L. The board wants revenue back to A$115m within two years and a view it can trust each month. Marcus believes hardware fell because of an industry soft patch (tariffs, interest rates, delayed venue projects, cautious OEMs), while software grew 38% to A$25.7m, gross margin rose from 71% to 77%, and there was a record 1,180 design wins. Q1 FY27 came in at about A$19m.

As Gary understands it: The fall itself is known. What's missing is one joined-up view that tells the board whether the business underneath is healthy or whether more falls are coming. Marcus has a story he wants the board to see, but by his own account: design-win conversion has never been measured, the CRM is bonus-driven, the forecasts aren't trusted, and the telemetry has never been used as an end-customer view. The dashboard has to test that story, not just tell it. The word to come back to is Marcus's own: "growing underneath the fall".

Total revenue, last 12 months, against the path to A$115m (K1)

Measure status: Corrected (hardware and other revenue split). Objective: Get revenue back to A$115m within two years (O1). Owner: CEO.

  • Plain English: Revenue over the last 12 months, shown each month against a straight line from FY26 actual (A$80.6m) to A$115m by June 2028. Split hardware and software.
  • Calculation: R12M(m) = -1 x SUM(gl_monthly.amount_aud) over the 12 months to month m, for account codes 4000, 4050, 4100, 4110, 4200, 4210. path(m) = 80.6m + (115m - 80.6m) x months since Jun-26 / 24. gap = R12M - path.
  • Sources and why: gl_monthly.amount_aud, period, account_code and gl_accounts.pl_group: the ledger Marcus trusts and the board pack uses. Cross-check: erp_invoice_lines.net_amount_aud by product_family reconciles to the ledger by financial year and family, largest difference A$0.00.
  • Grain: month by account (summed over cost centres).
  • Filters: pl_group 'revenue' (6 accounts); credits flipped to positive; volume rebates (4050, debit) reduce revenue and freight recovered (4210) is included. This reproduces A$118.4m (FY25) and A$80.6m (FY26) exactly. Hardware = 4000 + 4050. Software = 4100 + 4110. Other = 4200 + 4210 (developer kits and freight).
  • Baseline: A$80.54m for the 12 months to Sep-26 (hardware A$54.8m, software A$25.1m). Path at Sep-26: A$84.9m. Gap -A$4.36m (-5.14%), just beyond the 5% Red line. Observed Fact. Peak was A$122.1m in 2024-06.
  • Pace needed: (A$115m - A$80.54m) / 21 months to Jun-28 = A$1.64m a month. The straight-line path itself rises A$1.43m a month. Run rate over the last 3 months: -A$0.02m a month. Off pace.
  • Counter-metric: Gross margin %, last 12 months (K3.2), baseline 76.5%. Revenue can be pulled forward with volume rebates: in FY25, 46% of hardware revenue landed in the four quarter-end months (rebates are only ever booked in those months), against 33% if spread evenly. Watch the margin and the rebate rate (6.7% of hardware sales in FY25, 4.6% in FY26).
  • Notes: The R12M contains the A$7.4m licence of Mar-26 (9% of the figure). If the rest of FY27 repeats last year month by month without that licence, the 12 months to Jun-27 would be about A$73.1m, against a path of A$97.8m (an illustration, not a forecast). Hardware has been flat for five quarters (FY26-Q1 to FY27-Q1: A$12.9m, A$14.5m, A$12.6m, A$14.4m, A$13.4m). FY27-Q1 is +3.9% on the year before, the first quarter that is not a fall. No unmapped accounts, no duplicate ledger rows.

New devices activated in the field, last 12 months against the year before (K2)

Measure status: Corrected (repeat serials and OEM backfill). Objective: Know whether we're still growing underneath the fall (O1.1). Owner: VP Sales. Pending sign-off: Head of Platform Engineering (Tomasz Wierzbicki).

  • Plain English: Devices that joined a network for the first time in the last 12 months, against the 12 months before.
  • Calculation: keep the earliest first_seen_ts per device_serial; activations(R12M) = 10 x COUNT(device_serial) where that first date is in the 12 months to Sep-26; growth = this / prior 12 months - 1. The table is a 1-in-10 sample, so counts are multiplied by 10 (growth % is unaffected).
  • Sources and why: device_activations.device_serial, first_seen_ts (engineering telemetry, the source Marcus trusts most); oem_id, country_from_ip, module_sku for the splits.
  • Grain: device (first activation).
  • Filters: one row per serial (5,040 repeat rows dropped, 0.42% of rows); beta-firmware rows are all from 2021-22 and none fall in the window (0); data starts Jan-21; 107,210 rows have no oem_id (8.9%): 22,024 are recovered through manufacturer_code (each code maps to one OEM) and 85,186 stay unassigned. This affects only the OEM split, not the headline.
  • Baseline: -1.4% for the 12 months to Sep-26 (2,492,460 against 2,528,350). The year before was +3.0%. Observed Fact on the sample. Last quarter alone (Jul to Sep 26 against the same months of 2025): +4.9%.
  • Pace needed: (+5.0% - (-1.4%)) / 9 months to Jun-27 = +0.71 pts a month. Run rate: +0.63 pts a month. Slightly off pace, and closing.
  • Counter-metric: Share of activations that are repeat serials (a device re-joining counted as new), baseline 0.51% in the 12 months to Sep-26. It guards against re-flashed or test devices inflating the count.
  • Notes: Coverage is the open question (Q-4): the sample cannot say what share of installed devices phone home. Telemetry is a clue for end-customer demand, not yet proof. In the data, units invoiced per device activated were 1.91 in Jan to Jun 25 and 1.04 since Jul-25 (see the Root Cause Findings for what that implies). Duplicate-serial share is steady (0.47% before the dip, 0.53% during).

Sub-KPI under New devices activated: OEM stock on hand, top 40 OEMs (K2.1)

Measure status: Corrected (reels, free-text names, 35 reporters not 40). Owner: VP Sales. Pending the Head of Platform Engineering and VP Sales.

  • Plain English: Units of our parts the reporting OEMs hold, by calendar quarter, compared only across the OEMs that reported in both quarters.
  • Calculation: SUM(units) by quarter, where units = units_on_hand x 500 for rows described as reels of 500, otherwise units_on_hand. Quarter-on-quarter change uses only OEMs that reported in both quarters.
  • Sources and why: oem_inventory_reports.oem_name, quarter, sku_or_description, units_on_hand (Sales; the only stock view we have); oem_master.oem_name to match names.
  • Grain: OEM by quarter by SKU line.
  • Filters: 235 spellings of OEM names clean up to 35 OEMs (case, spacing, hyphens and company suffixes), and all match oem_master. 88 rows are reels of 500 (counts of 3 to 117, converted to units). 229 rows are finished goods containing our parts: kept, but they are not loose stock. No duplicate rows. Calendar quarters.
