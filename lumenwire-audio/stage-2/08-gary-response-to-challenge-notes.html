From Gary to Adam and Kewpie. 26-Oct-26. Sample data. This answers the 6 corrections and 15 challenge notes in 2026.10.26 - Lumenwire Audio - 02 Challenge Notes.md. I haven't changed the Business Requirements. Every change to a KPI, target or threshold below is a proposal waiting for Adam's sign-off. Every new target and threshold is my proposal, labelled Working Hypothesis, unless it says otherwise.

The short version. Kewpie's numbers test Marcus's story, which is what the dashboard is for. Three things he believed don't hold up: software growth is one licence, about a third of design wins ship (not 80%), and hardware fell mostly because OEMs stopped buying ahead of installs, not because end demand fell. My response keeps all five KPIs. I've proposed new targets where the old ones fail the Attainable test, and I've added "with and without the one-off licence" wherever that licence distorts the picture.
