A3Months of stock cover at the OEMs that report (K2.1)Divisor left open: "average monthly units activated or invoiced (Kewpie to confirm)"; stock "SUM(units_on_hand, reels x 500)" at reporting OEMsDivisor = average monthly units invoiced to the same OEMs in the same quarter (erp_invoice_lines.qty, chip, module, card). Activations are a 1-in-10 sample with 7% of devices unassigned. Result in 2026-Q3: 10.9 months (Gary's draft said 9.3).
A4Software revenue growth (K3)Formula (v1.1 draft): "leaving out any single licence worth more than A$1m (threshold to agree with the CFO)" with the calculation -1 * SUM(gl_monthly.amount_aud) for accounts 4100 and 4110, R12M over prior R12M, minus 1 (no exclusion in the calculation)Calculation now includes the exclusion: (software R12M less licences over A$1m started in R12M) / (software prior R12M less licences over A$1m started in prior R12M) - 1. Growth including them is shown alongside.The calculation had to match the plain English. Result -15.6% (1 licence removed).
A5Design wins that ship (K4)Calculation: "COUNT(opp where stage_entered_shipping not blank) / COUNT(opp where stage_entered_design_win <= today - 18 months)"; Sources include "erp_invoice_lines.oem_id, sku (cross-check)"Same ratio, with 'today' fixed at the end of the data (30-Sep-26), shipped defined by the shipping date, and rows with no stage dates excluded and counted. The invoice cross-check is dropped for lack of a key.A stage field without dates cannot be placed in a cohort. OEM-level invoices cannot confirm a product shipped: nearly every OEM buys every year.
A6Months from design win to first shipment (K4.1)Calculation: "MEDIAN(months_between(stage_entered_design_win, stage_entered_shipping))" for opportunities that shippedSame median, restricted to design wins at least 18 months old.Younger cohorts only show their fastest shippers. Median 15.0 months for mature cohorts against 14.3 for all.
A7Forecast accuracy (K5)Calculation: "ABS(actual_q - forecast_q) / actual_q; forecast_q = forecast_snapshots.forecast_amount from the snapshot nearest the quarter start"error = (forecast - actual) / actual, reported as a signed figure and an absolute figure. Forecast = latest snapshot on or before the first day of the quarter, converted to AUD, summed over hardware, software and other. Actual = hardware (4000, 4050), software (4100, 4110), other (4200, 4210).'Nearest' could pick a snapshot taken after the quarter began. The sign shows bias. The family mapping matches the board plan.
